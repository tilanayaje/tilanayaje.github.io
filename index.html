Urban Traffic Flow Optimizer

Capstone

2025–2026

Anyone who has sat through the same red light twice has wondered whether the timing could be better. Twenty intersections, forty signal-timing genes, and a genetic algorithm looking for a better plan. Scoring one candidate means running a whole SUMO simulation, so a single generation is twelve simulations.

Four of us on the capstone. I wrote the GA, the TraCI integration and the harness that runs those twelve simulations at the same time. That last piece is why a twenty-generation search takes about five hours instead of an estimated forty.

  • 12.7%lower mean wait, 85.3s to 74.5s
  • 5.5%higher throughput
  • t = 13.20Welch's t-test, 20 runs each, p < 10⁻⁶
  • 92%final capstone grade

Python, SUMO, TraCI, PyGAD, multiprocessing, Streamlit

DroneTreeCV

Computer vision

2025

Point a camera at a tree and it tells you which of ten Ontario species you're looking at. I pulled about 49,000 photos off the iNaturalist API, deduplicated them by hash, and fine-tuned YOLOv8l on my own 4060 Ti. It took three runs to get from 78.8% to 92.3%, mostly by reading the confusion matrix and going back for more data on whichever species were getting mixed up.

On live video the prediction flickered between species every frame. Temporal smoothing settled it, with a confidence threshold and nearest-neighbour retrieval over 24,500 backbone embeddings sitting behind it.

Never flown. It is trained and tested on ground-level photographs, and I have not tested it on a real drone yet.

  • 92.3%top-1 accuracy
  • 10,736image held-out set
  • 49,000images collected

Python, PyTorch, YOLOv8, CUDA, OpenCV

One Day

Cross-platform app

2026

Most habit apps punish you for missing a day. This one shows a 365-day map slowly filling in, sets goals by the week, and marks the 66-day mark where Lally's research says the habit stops costing you effort. One React Native codebase runs as a web app and as a Windows build through Electron, and guest mode lets you use all of it before making an account.

I didn't want people's habit data sitting in plaintext on a server I control. A Supabase Edge Function checks your token and derives a per-user key with HKDF, then the app encrypts with AES-256-GCM before anything syncs, so the database only ever holds ciphertext. The charts are all drawn by hand, no library.

  • 365day map, drawn without a charting library
  • 66days to formed, per Lally's research
  • 256bit AES-GCM, applied before anything syncs

React Native, Expo, Electron, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Google OAuth, Vercel

PitchTrainer

Real-time audio

2026

I started singing and wanted something that would tell me when I was flat. Pick a drill and a tonic, it plays the phrase, counts you in, listens while you sing it back, and scores every note in cents. I wrote the pitch detection myself, autocorrelation with parabolic interpolation and a median smoother, and moved it into an AudioWorklet because the main thread on a phone drops enough frames to miss a short note. No framework, no bundler, no build step.

Notes I'd sung correctly kept coming back as misses. It turned out to be about 58ms of group delay between the worklet buffer and the smoother, which meant every sample was being matched against the wrong moment in the drill. Subtracting it fixed the scoring.

  • 58msof detection latency compensated for
  • 0frameworks, bundlers or build steps

JavaScript, Web Audio API, AudioWorklet, Canvas

Evermore Events

Production website

2026

Two owners in Windsor needed a site that brought them work and didn't need me every time something changed. Quote requests come in through a Supabase RPC behind row-level security and land in an admin panel they run themselves. They edit or hide décor items, hit publish, and that fires a Cloudflare rebuild.

I tested the schema against a live database before launch, which caught an RLS policy that was quietly dropping line items off anonymous submissions. Nobody would have noticed that until a real customer's quote came through half empty.

Energy Savings Solutions

Landing page

2026

A client starting an HVAC sales business needed a site, though not for the reason you'd expect. The CRM I was building him needed outbound calling, and Twilio won't issue a number without a company URL, so the site had to go out first to unblock everything else.

The CRM is the part he actually came to me for: somewhere to keep his leads, work orders that generate themselves as PDFs, and calling with recording built in through Twilio, on Node and Postgres with a React frontend. Still in progress.

Six service categories, a four-step process, and a rebates section I wrote carefully: it says which programs a job might qualify for, then states plainly that Energy Savings Solutions isn't a government agency or utility and doesn't administer or guarantee any public rebate. An HVAC business implying otherwise is a real liability, not a copy nitpick. Structured data in the head marks it up as an HVACBusiness for local search.

  • 6service categories, from heat pumps to electrical capacity
  • 4step process laid out before a customer is asked to commit

HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript · CRM: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, React, Twilio Voice API
